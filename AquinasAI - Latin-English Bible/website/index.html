Frequently Asked Questions

What is Verbum Dei?

Verbum Dei is a comprehensive Catholic Bible and prayer app that provides access to all 73 books of the Catholic Bible in three languages: Latin (Vulgate), English (Catholic Public Domain Version), and Spanish (Reina-Valera). The app features parallel text display, traditional Catholic prayers, rosary guide, and biblical study tools.

What languages does the app support?

Verbum Dei supports three languages: Latin (complete Vulgate text), English (Catholic Public Domain Version), and Spanish (Reina-Valera translation). The app offers six display modes including single language views and bilingual parallel text comparisons (Latin-English, Latin-Spanish, English-Spanish).

Is the Latin Vulgate included?

Yes, Verbum Dei includes the complete Latin Vulgate Bible with all 73 Catholic canonical books. The Latin text can be read independently or displayed side-by-side with English or Spanish translations for parallel study and linguistic comparison.

Does it include Catholic prayers?

Yes, Verbum Dei includes a comprehensive collection of traditional Catholic prayers in Latin and English, including the complete Rosary with all mysteries (Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, and Luminous), Divine Mercy Chaplet, Order of Mass, and classic prayers like the Ave Maria, Pater Noster, and Gloria Patri.

What devices is it available on?

Verbum Dei is available on iOS devices (iPhone and iPad) running iOS 15.0 or later, and on Android devices. The app supports both smartphones and tablets, with responsive design optimized for various screen sizes. Download from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
